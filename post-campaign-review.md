# Post-Campaign Review Guide

A practical guide for reviewing campaign results and
driving improvements based on hands-on experience as IT Project Ops.

> Tools covered: CleverTap, MoEngage, Stamps CRM System

---

## Overview

Post-campaign review is a collaborative process between
ops, data team, marketing team, and client. Ops plays a
key role in bridging campaign results with system capabilities
— translating data insights into actionable next steps
within platform limitations.

---

## Review Process

### Step 1 — Data Collection
- Data and marketing team pulls campaign metrics
- Metrics typically reviewed:
  - Sent count & delivery rate
  - Open rate (push / email)
  - Click-through rate (CTR)
  - Conversion rate
  - Revenue impact (if tracked)
  - Journey drop-off points (for automated journeys)

### Step 2 — Results Presentation to Client
- [ ] Prepare campaign summary report
- [ ] Present results to client with context:
  - What performed well
  - What underperformed
  - Comparison to previous campaigns (if available)
- [ ] Gather client feedback on results

### Step 3 — Brainstorming & Improvement (if underperforming)
- [ ] Conduct internal discussion with marketing team
- [ ] Identify root cause of underperformance:
  - Wrong segmentation?
  - Wrong timing?
  - Content not engaging enough?
  - Feature limitation?
- [ ] Brainstorm alternative campaign approaches
- [ ] Align on next campaign strategy with client

---

## Ops Role in Post-Campaign

As IT Project Ops, the key responsibilities in this phase are:

**1. Feature Clarification**
When client or marketing team requests campaign features
that are not yet available in the system, ops explains
current platform capabilities and limitations clearly.

**2. Alternative Solution Finding**
If a requested feature is not available, ops works with
the marketing team and client to find the best alternative
solution within existing system capabilities.

**3. Feature Request Documentation**
If a requested feature is valid and recurring, ops documents
it as a feature request for the dev team to consider
in future development.

---

## Common Scenarios

| Scenario | Ops Action |
|----------|------------|
| Client requests unavailable feature | Explain limitation, propose alternative |
| Campaign underperformed | Join brainstorm, suggest segmentation or timing fix |
| Journey drop-off too high | Review journey logic, propose flow adjustment |
| Client wants new campaign type | Check platform capability, align with marketing team |
| Feature request from client | Document and escalate to dev team for future consideration |
